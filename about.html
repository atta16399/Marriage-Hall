About us

About Marriage Hall

Marriage Hall is a matrimonial application designed to help people discover compatible marriage prospects and communicate with suitable users.

What We're Building

Marriage Hall exists to make it easier to find a compatible matrimonial match. Instead of an unfiltered stream of profiles, the app is organized around the preferences each person sets — so what you see is shaped by what actually matters to you in a partner.

The app gives users tools to build a profile, search and filter prospects, message people they're interested in, and manage their own privacy and safety through blocking and reporting.

Our focus

Preferences, not noise

Discovery is shaped by what you tell Marriage Hall matters to you.

Your safety tools

Blocking and reporting, built in

Manage who can reach you and flag profiles that break the rules.

What matters to us

Our Approach

Compatibility First

Profile and preference data are used to surface relevant prospects rather than random browsing.

User Control

People manage their own profile visibility, preferences, and who can contact them.

Straightforward Design

The app is built to be simple to use, from creating a profile to starting a conversation.
