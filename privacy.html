Privacy Policy for Marriage Hall App

Introduction

Marriage Hall App (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring a safe community. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

By using the Marriage Hall App, you confirm that you are 18 years of age or older and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create a profile, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Age (18+ only)
  • Gender
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Voluntary personal details you choose to add

1.2 Non-Personal Information

We may automatically collect certain non-identifiable information, such as:

  • Device details (model, operating system, version)
  • App usage statistics
  • IP address and general location

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide matchmaking and communication features
  • Enable secure in-app chat between users
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Enforce community safety rules and Terms of Service

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share your information only with:

  • Service providers (e.g., hosting, security, analytics) who support our app operations
  • Authorities if required by law, or when necessary to protect users from harm or abuse

4. Data Security

We apply industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee 100% protection against unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have the following rights:

  • Access and Edit: Update your profile information at any time
  • Delete Account: Permanently remove your account and associated data
  • Revoke Permissions: Control app permissions through your device settings

6. Age Restriction

  • The Marriage Hall App is strictly for adults aged 18 and above.
  • We do not knowingly collect or store information from children under 18.
  • If we discover that a user under 18 has registered, their account will be removed immediately.

7. Prohibited Content & Child Safety Standards

To comply with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards, our app explicitly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM/CSAE)
  • Exploitation, harassment, or harmful interactions involving minors
  • Any inappropriate or abusive content

We have reporting mechanisms in place to handle safety concerns.

8. Third-Party Links

Our app may include links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy or safety practices of third-party services.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted in the app and/or on our official page. Please review this policy regularly.
